## 3. Economic Profit, DCF & SOTP Tear-Down of `Project Alpine` (`REJECT`)
1. **Severe Economic Profit Destruction (`ROIC << WACC`)**: Even accepting independent normalized post-synergy EBIT of `€100.80M` (`€75.60M` NOPAT at `25.0%` tax), steady-state post-tax **ROIC on the `€1,800.0M` headline EV is `4.20%`**—trailing EuroNet's `7.80%` WACC by **`-360 bps`** and destroying **`-€64.80M/year`** of Economic Profit (`Invested Capital × (ROIC - WACC)`). Moreover, borrowing `€1,800.0M` at `6.85%` (`€123.30M/yr` pre-tax interest, `5.14%` after-tax) exceeds asset ROIC (`4.20%`) and consumes `78.8%` of Helvetia's `€156.52M` LTM EBITDA before CapEx or severance.
2. **Massive Negative DCF Net Present Value**: Discounting explicit Years 1–5 unlevered FCFs (`[€42.0M, €68.0M, €82.0M, €85.0M, €86.0M]`, PV = `€284.93M`) plus Year-6 terminal FCF (`€88.58M` at `1.0%` perpetual growth -> `€1,302.65M` Year-5 TV; PV = `€894.84M`) at `7.80%` WACC yields a **Standalone Intrinsic PV of `€1,179.77M`** (`7.54x` LTM EBITDA). Paying `€1,800.0M` (`11.50x`) inflicts an immediate **`-€620.23M` Base NPV loss** (**`-€825.23M` Fully-Loaded NPV loss** on `€2,005.0M` all-in cost).
3. **SOTP "Best Natural Owner" Failure & Covenant Contagion**: Paying `11.50x` for an IT outsourcer where **`68.0%` of revenue is legacy mainframe, Solaris, and MPLS shrinking at `-6.5%/year`** and **`41.0%` of revenue sits in Top-5 bank contracts expiring in 2027** compounds EuroNet's legacy copper/MPLS runoff risk rather than creating cloud moat. Pro-forma RCF Net Leverage leaps to **`3.34x`** (`€7,026.0M / €2,106.52M`; **`3.43x`** all-in on `€7,231.0M`), breaching the `2.50x` RCF covenant by `+0.84x` (`+0.93x` all-in) and risking `€1,500.0M` RCF acceleration.
