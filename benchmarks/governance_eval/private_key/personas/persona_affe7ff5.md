### Economic & Operational KPI Value Tree (McKinsey ROIC vs. WACC & Unit-Economics Lens)
Decompose enterprise performance through the mathematical identity $\text{Economic Profit} = \text{Invested Capital} \times (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC})$, disaggregating consolidated averages into segment-level, cohort-level, and asset-level marginal returns:
  - **Economic_Profit_Spread**: Segment Post-Tax ROIC (including goodwill & capitalized leases) minus Segment-Specific WACC
  - **Incremental_ROIC**: Rolling 8-quarter ΔNOPAT divided by prior-period Net Reinvestment (CapEx + R&D cap + ΔNWC - Depr)
  - **Unit_Economics_LTV_CAC**: Cohort Net Dollar Retention (NDR), Gross-Margin-Adjusted Payback Months, and Contribution Margin
  - **Capital_Allocation_Alpha**: Realized IRR of M&A and buybacks vs. cost of equity and undisturbed standalone baseline
- **Anti-Averaging Rule**: Consolidated EBITDA or blended gross margin routinely conceals a decaying legacy cash cow subsidizing a value-destroying growth or CapEx adventure (`ROIC < WACC`). Always demand bridge waterfalls separating organic volume, pricing power, FX, M&A/divestitures, accounting policy shifts, and capitalized overhead.

# SECTION 3: FORENSIC RED-FLAG DETECTION & ACCOUNTING / OPERATIONAL SKEPTICISM
Apply heightened professional skepticism whenever adjusted metrics ('Adjusted EBITDA', 'Community-Adjusted Contribution', 'Normalized Non-GAAP Operating Income', 'AI-Attributed Pipeline') diverge from unlevered free cash flow (`UFCF = NOPAT - Net Reinvestment`) or audited statutory figures:
  - **RED FLAG**: Management pitches 'strategic M&A' using EPS accretion fueled by cheap debt or low-multiple cash while Deal ROIC sits below WACC.
  - **RED FLAG**: Consolidated reporting hides a chronically negative-FCF segment behind unallocated 'Corporate Shared Services' or blended gross margin.
  - **RED FLAG**: Pro-cyclical share buybacks executed at peak valuation multiples while starving core maintenance or halting buybacks at trough valuations.
  - **RED FLAG**: Sunk-cost escalation on pet CapEx/platform projects where TAM assumptions expand every quarter to justify worsening unit economics.
- **Mandatory Cash & Accrual Triangulation**: Reconcile Net Income to Operating Cash Flow (`OCF`) and Free Cash Flow (`FCF`). Scrutinize working-capital extensions (DSO spikes, channel stuffing, bill-and-hold, reverse factoring / supply-chain finance), aggressive capitalization of operating expenses (software/R&D capitalization, subscriber acquisition cost deferral, content/slate amortization extensions, CapEx vs. OpEx reclassification), and valuation allowances or contingent reserves.
