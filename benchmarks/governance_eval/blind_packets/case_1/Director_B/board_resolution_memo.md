## 2. Forensic Accounting Restatements & Senior RCF Covenant Audit

### A. `IAS 38.57` & `IAS 16.12` Reversal — Note 7 ("Aurora" Capitalization Policy Override)
Effective 1 January 2026, management reclassified **`€310.0M`** of recurring operating expenses into eCapEx via CFO manual journal override: **`€185.0M`** of Tier-2/3 legacy OSS/BSS bug-fix and ticket maintenance plus **`€125.0M`** of weather-related copper-drop fault truck-roll repairs. Internal engineering logs confirm `0%` new software capability creation and `0%` asset useful-life extension.
- **Accounting Mandate**: Under `IAS 38.57`, software expenditure cannot be capitalized without demonstrated technical feasibility and incremental future economic benefits; under `IAS 16.12`, day-to-day servicing and repairs of network plant must be expensed as incurred.
- **P&L and Cash Flow Impact**: Reversing the `€310.0M` override increases OpEx (`€5,620.0M -> €5,930.0M`), reduces FY2026 Normalized EBITDA from `€2,260.0M` (`28.68%` margin) to **`€1,950.0M` (`24.75%` margin, `-5.61%` YoY)**, lowers OCF by `€310.0M` (`€2,039.0M -> €1,729.0M` pre-factoring), and restores eCapEx from `€1,480.0M` to **`€1,170.0M`** (matching FY2025; FCF-neutral on Note 7 alone, exposing WorldCom-style EBITDA/OCF inflation).

### B. `IAS 7` / `IFRS 7` Reclassification — Note 11 (Novated Supplier Reverse Factoring)
Management excluded **`€480.0M`** of FY2026 obligations (`€320.0M` in FY2025) to Alpine Trade Bank AG from Gross Debt, parking them in *"Other Working Capital Accruals"* while booking the **`+€160.0M`** YoY balance expansion inside OCF.
- **Substance Over Form**: A tri-party novation extinguishes vendor trade payables at Day 60 and creates a `EURIBOR + 265 bps` interest-bearing bank debt obligation due at Day 180 (`+120` days past standard `60-day` trade terms). Under `IAS 7` / `IFRS 7` Supplier Finance amendments and **Clause 22.4 of the `€1,500.0M` Senior RCF** (which includes novated obligations or terms extended `>90` days past 60-day industry terms), the entire `€480.0M` facility is financial debt, and the `€160.0M` net expansion is a Financing Cash Flow (`CFF`) borrowing, not OCF.

## 3. Binding Rejection & Economic Profit Tear-Down of *Project Alpine* (`2026-Q4-M&A-01`)

**Board Resolution**: **REJECT `2026-Q4-M&A-01` (`Helvetia Legacy IT Services AG`)**. Paying `€1,800.0M` (`11.50x` LTM EBITDA of `€156.52M`) funded 100% with `6.85%` Bridge-to-Bond debt violates capital-allocation discipline and solvency limits on four independent grounds:

1. **Structural Value Destruction (`ROIC` vs. `WACC` & Economic Profit)**: Independent post-synergy steady-state EBIT of `€100.80M` generates **`€75.60M` NOPAT** (`25.0%` tax rate), equating to **`4.20%` ROIC** on `€1,800.0M` Purchase EV—a **`-360 bps` value-destructive spread** below EuroNet's **`7.80%` WACC** (`-€64.80M` annual Economic Profit deficit). Including `€95.0M` of unfunded `IAS 19` pension/works-council liabilities and `€110.0M` of required integration/severance cash outlays (`€2,005.0M` fully-loaded outlay), ROIC falls to **`3.77%`** (`-403 bps` below WACC).
2. **Severe Negative DCF Net Present Value**: Discounting explicit Year 1–5 unlevered FCFs (`€42.0M, €68.0M, €82.0M, €85.0M, €86.0M`; `PV = €284.94M`) plus Year-5 Gordon Growth Terminal Value (`€88.58M / (7.80% - 1.00%) = €1,302.65M`; `PV = €894.83M`) at `7.80%` WACC yields an **Intrinsic Enterprise Value (`PV`) of `€1,179.77M`** (`7.54x` EBITDA). Executing at `€1,800.0M` immediately destroys **`-€620.23M` of shareholder NPV** (**`-€825.23M` fully-loaded NPV** inclusive of `€95.0M` pension and `€110.0M` integration/severance obligations).
3. **Melting-Ice-Cube Commercial Risk & Interest Coverage Deficit**: `68%` of Helvetia's revenue derives from decaying mainframe, Solaris, and MPLS hosting eroding at **`-6.5%` annually**, while **`41%` of revenue sits in top-5 bank contracts expiring in 2027**. Annual interest on `€1,800.0M` new debt at `6.85%` (`€123.30M/yr` pre-tax) exceeds Helvetia's normalized post-synergy EBIT (`€100.80M`), making the acquisition cash-flow deficit-producing before principal amortization.
4. **Immediate RCF Event of Default**: Adding `€1,800.0M` debt (`€7,026.0M` Pro-Forma Net Debt) against `€2,106.52M` combined LTM EBITDA (`€1,950.0M + €156.52M`) drives **Pro-Forma Net Leverage to `3.34x`** (**`3.43x`** fully loaded at `€7,231.0M`)—`+0.84x` above the `2.50x` RCF covenant limit (even on management's unadjusted figures, leverage would hit `2.71x`, still breaching `2.50x`).
