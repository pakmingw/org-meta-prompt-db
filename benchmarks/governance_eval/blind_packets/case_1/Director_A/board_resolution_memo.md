## 2. Forensic Accounting Restatement & Senior RCF Covenant Audit (`IAS 38`, `IAS 7 / IFRS 7`)
Reconciling statutory cash conservation against management's adjusted disclosures exposes three material misstatements:

1. **Aurora Policy Override (`IAS 38.57` Violation — `-€310.0M` EBITDA Restatement)**: Effective January 1, 2026, a manual CFO journal override bypassed engineering feasibility gates to reclassify **`€310.0M`** of recurring maintenance from OpEx into eCapEx (`€185.0M` Tier-2/3 legacy OSS/BSS bugfix/ticket support and `€125.0M` copper weather-fault truck-roll repairs). Internal engineering logs verify `0%` new feature creation and `0%` asset useful-life extension. Under `IAS 38.57` and Senior RCF **Clause 22.4**, routine maintenance must be expensed immediately. Reversing this override increases FY2026 OpEx from `€5,620.0M` to `€5,930.0M`, reduces true eCapEx from `€1,480.0M` back to `€1,170.0M`, and restates FY2026 Normalized EBITDA from `€2,260.0M` (`28.68%` margin, `+9.40%` YoY) to **`€1,950.0M` (`24.75%` margin, `-5.61%` YoY vs. FY2025 `€2,065.8M`)**.
2. **Tri-Party Supplier Reverse Factoring (`IAS 7 / IFRS 7` Violation — `-€160.0M` FCF & `+€480.0M` Debt Reclassification)**: Under Note 11, vendor payables (`€890.0M` standard 60-day terms remain valid working capital) were extinguished at Day 60 via tri-party novation with Alpine Trade Bank AG and converted into **180-day interest-bearing bank obligations (`EURIBOR + 265 bps`)**—`120` days past normal 60-day industry terms—hidden inside `'Other Working Capital Accruals'`. Expanding this facility from `€320.0M` (FY2025) to **`€480.0M` (FY2026)** injected **`€160.0M`** of artificial working-capital cash inflow into Reported OCF (`€2,039.0M`). Stripping out both the `€310.0M` Aurora OCF/eCapEx reclassification wash and the `€160.0M` financing inflow reduces True Normalized OCF to **`€1,569.0M`** and True Underlying FCF from `€559.0M` (`-14.00%` YoY) to **`€399.0M` (`-38.62%` YoY vs. FY2025 `€650.0M`)**.
3. **Active Pre-M&A Breach of the `€1,500.0M` Senior RCF Maintenance Covenant (`Clause 22.4`)**: Clause 22.4 mandates inclusion of novated supplier-finance facilities extended `>90` days past 60-day terms in Consolidated Net Debt while excluding capitalized routine maintenance from Consolidated EBITDA. Adding the `€480.0M` Alpine Trade Bank facility to `€5,420.0M` Reported Gross Debt yields `€5,900.0M` Adjusted Gross Debt; less `€674.0M` unrestricted cash, **True Adjusted Net Debt is `€5,226.0M`** (vs. `€4,746.0M` reported). Dividing `€5,226.0M` by `€1,950.0M` Normalized EBITDA yields **`2.68x` Adjusted Net Leverage**—**breaching the `2.50x` RCF covenant ceiling by `+0.18x` (`€351.0M` excess debt)** *before* borrowing a single euro for *Project Alpine*.

## 3. Economic Profit (`ROIC vs. WACC`), DCF & SOTP Tear-Down of *Project Alpine*
Applying strict economic-profit discipline ($\text{Economic Profit} = \text{Invested Capital} \times (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC})$) and Best-Natural-Owner SOTP screening, *Project Alpine* (`Helvetia Legacy IT Services AG`) is a textbook value-destroying empire-building trap:

- **Sub-WACC ROIC & Annual Economic Profit Destruction**: Even accepting independent steady-state post-synergy EBIT of `€100.80M` (`€75.60M` NOPAT at `25%` tax), *Project Alpine* generates a **`4.20%` Headline ROIC** on `€1,800.0M` Purchase EV (`11.50x` LTM EBITDA of `€156.52M`)—trailing EuroNet's **`7.80%` WACC by `-360 bps`** and destroying **`-€64.80M` of Economic Profit every year**. Including due-diligence liabilities omitted from management's headline pitch—**`€95.0M` unfunded `IAS 19` pension/works-council obligations** plus **`€110.0M` upfront integration and severance outlays** (**`€2,005.0M` All-In Invested Capital**, `12.81x` effective EBITDA)—compresses **All-In ROIC to `3.77%` (`-403 bps` spread)**, destroying **`-€80.80M/yr`** in Economic Profit.
- **Intrinsic DCF Valuation Deficit (`-€620.23M` Headline / `-€825.23M` All-In NPV)**: Discounting explicit Years 1–5 unlevered FCFs (`€42.0M, €68.0M, €82.0M, €85.0M, €86.0M` $\rightarrow$ PV **`€284.95M`**) plus Year-6 terminal FCF (`€88.58M` at `1.0%` perpetual growth $\rightarrow$ `TV_5 = €1,302.65M`, PV **`€894.82M`**) at `7.80%` WACC yields an Intrinsic Enterprise Value (`PV`) of **`€1,179.77M`** (`7.54x` LTM EBITDA). Paying `€1,800.0M` transfers **`€620.23M`** of EuroNet shareholder wealth directly to Helvetia's sellers (**`-€825.23M` all-in NPV** after `€205.0M` pension and severance obligations).
- **Negative Cash Carry, Covenant Insolvency & SOTP Mismatch**: Financing `€1,800.0M` at `6.85%` (`€123.30M/yr` pre-tax interest; `€92.48M` after-tax) exceeds Helvetia's steady-state EBIT (`€100.80M`) and creates a `-€50.48M` Year-1 cash deficit against `€42.0M` Year-1 unlevered FCF before `€110.0M` severance outlays. Pro-forma Net Leverage surges to **`3.34x` (`€7,026.0M / €2,106.52M`)** on headline debt and **`3.43x` (`€7,231.0M / €2,106.52M`)** all-in—nearly a full turn above the `2.50x` RCF cap. Commercially, **`68%`** of Helvetia's revenue sits in melting mainframe/Solaris/MPLS infrastructure (`-6.5%` annual attrition) and **`41%`** of top-5 bank contracts expire in 2027, rendering management's `"€65M Year-3 AI cross-sell synergy"` pitch unsubstantiated. EuroNet is not the Best Natural Owner of a shrinking legacy IT outsourcer.
